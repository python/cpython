Speed up ``in``, :meth:`memoryview.count` and :meth:`memoryview.index` for
:class:`memoryview` objects with integer formats. Attempts to resize during
these operations may now raise :exc:`BufferError`. Searching is 60-130x faster
for 100,000 items. :meth:`memoryview.index` now raises
:exc:`NotImplementedError` for an unsupported format even when there are no
items to search, like :meth:`~memoryview.count` and ``in``.
