Make ``await`` of a coroutine that completes without suspending about twice as
fast by running it without creating a coroutine object. This also applies to
``async def`` :meth:`~object.__aenter__` and :meth:`~object.__aexit__` methods
in :keyword:`async with`. Until such a coroutine
first suspends, :attr:`frame.f_generator` is ``None``.
