Fix :func:`TypeError` not being raised for item assignment or deletion on
types that do not support it (such as :class:`set`, :class:`tuple`,
:class:`str`, :class:`bytes`, :class:`range` and :class:`frozenset`) when
the index is too large to fit in a :c:type:`Py_ssize_t`.  Previously an
incorrect :exc:`IndexError` was raised.  Patch by TINGyu123644.
