:meth:`ssl.SSLContext.wrap_bio` now validates its *server_side*,
*server_hostname* and *session* arguments similar to
:meth:`ssl.SSLContext.wrap_socket`.

In particular, a context with :attr:`~ssl.SSLContext.check_hostname` enabled
and no *server_hostname* passed to :meth:`!wrap_bio` now raises :exc:`ValueError`
instead of completing a handshake that verified the certificate chain
without verifying the peer's identity, with no indication that the
check had been skipped.
