:meth:`ssl.SSLContext.wrap_bio` now validates its *server_side*,
*server_hostname* and *session* arguments similar to
:meth:`ssl.SSLContext.wrap_socket`, but for backward compatiblity reasons
emits :exc:`DeprecationWarning` instead of :exc:`ValueError`.

In particular, a context with :attr:`~ssl.SSLContext.check_hostname` enabled
and no *server_hostname* passed to :meth:`!wrap_bio` now emits
:exc:`DeprecationWarning` to indicate the hostname wasn't checked.
(In Python 3.13 and later, this raises :exc:`ValueError`.)
