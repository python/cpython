Fix a crash in :mod:`ssl` when an :attr:`~ssl.SSLContext.sni_callback`
switches a connection to another :class:`~ssl.SSLContext` and the context
that carries the callback is no longer referenced by the application.
Servers that keep their ``sni_callback`` context alive (the usual case when
it wraps the listening socket or is stored on the server object) were not
affected.
This addresses :cve:`2026-19445`.
