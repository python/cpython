Work around a macOS and FreeBSD kernel bug that could make
:meth:`subprocess.Popen.communicate` hang or block past its *timeout*.
When the operating system is under system-wide pipe buffer memory pressure,
:func:`select.poll` and :func:`select.select` can report the child's stdin
pipe as writable when a write to it would block. This was most likely to be
seen on loaded hosts with many pipes in use at once. :meth:`!communicate`
now keeps stdin non-blocking while it writes to it and retries after a short
delay when the pipe turns out to be full.
