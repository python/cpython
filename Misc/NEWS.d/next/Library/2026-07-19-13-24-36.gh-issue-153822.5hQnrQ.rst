Introduced structure for keeping raw header with whitespaces, to properly recreate headers for payloads that are PGP signed.
Raw header value overrides the default header folding of :meth:`email.policy.EmailPolicy.fold` and :meth:`email.policy.Compat32.fold` in :meth:`email.generator.Generator._write_headers` and bytes equivalents, preserving message and allowing for successfull signature validation. Contributed by Michał Karol.
