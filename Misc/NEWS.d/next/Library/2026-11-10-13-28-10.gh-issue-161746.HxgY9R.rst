Speed up conversion of ordinals to dates in :mod:`datetime` by switching to
the `Neri–Schneider algorithm <https://arxiv.org/abs/2102.06959>`__.
:meth:`~datetime.date.fromordinal` is about 30% faster, and
:meth:`~datetime.date.fromisocalendar` and :class:`~datetime.timedelta`
arithmetic across month boundaries are about 10–20% faster.
