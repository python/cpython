Fix :exc:`AttributeError` from the methods of an :mod:`asyncio` SSL transport,
such as :meth:`~asyncio.WriteTransport.write` and
:meth:`~asyncio.BaseTransport.set_protocol`, when the transport is closed after
its connection was lost. This also affected the fallback of
:meth:`loop.sendfile() <asyncio.loop.sendfile>` over SSL when the peer went
away mid-transfer. The SSL context is still released once the connection is
lost.
