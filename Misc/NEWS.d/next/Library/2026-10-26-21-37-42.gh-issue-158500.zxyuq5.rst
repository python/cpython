:meth:`datetime.date.fromisoformat` now raises :exc:`ValueError` for a
basic-format date followed by extra characters, such as ``'2020010112'``,
instead of silently ignoring them. The pure-Python implementation of
:meth:`datetime.datetime.fromisoformat` now also rejects a date followed by a
single character, such as ``'2020-01-01T'``, matching the C implementation.
