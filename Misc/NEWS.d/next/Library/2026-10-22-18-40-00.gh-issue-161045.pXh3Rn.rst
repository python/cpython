Fix :mod:`tarfile` writing the old name, link name, owner, size or
modification time of a member that was read from an archive with a pax
extended header and then changed, for example with
:meth:`tarfile.TarInfo.replace`, before it was added to a
:const:`~tarfile.PAX_FORMAT` archive.
A ``hdrcharset=BINARY`` field that was read from an archive is no longer
written in front of fields that are encoded in UTF-8.
