Fix exponential matching time in :func:`glob.translate`,
:meth:`pathlib.PurePath.match`, :meth:`~pathlib.PurePath.full_match` and
:meth:`pathlib.Path.glob` for patterns with several ``*`` wildcards in one
segment. Interior ``*`` wildcards are now translated to atomic groups where
this cannot change which names match, as :func:`fnmatch.translate` does.
