Fix :meth:`compression.zstd.ZstdDecompressor.decompress` raising an error when
called repeatedly with ``max_length=0``. Calling ``decompress()`` with
``max_length=0`` on the Zstandard decompressor, :class:`bz2.BZ2Decompressor`
and :class:`!zlib._ZlibDecompressor` now always buffers the input and returns
``b''`` without decompressing anything.
