Applied changes to ``importlib.metadata`` from `importlib_metadata 10.0
<https://importlib-metadata.readthedocs.io/en/latest/history.html#v10-0-0>`_:
added the ``IDistribution`` and ``IPackagePath`` protocols, so a consumer can
target one interface across ``importlib.metadata`` and the backport rather
than either library's concrete classes, and the functions returning
distributions and files are annotated with them.
