Fix a data race in :class:`functools.partial` in free-threaded builds.
Mutating a partial shared between threads with ``__setstate__()``, as
:func:`copy.copy` and :mod:`pickle` do, could crash the interpreter when
another thread read it at the same time, and a reader could combine the
arguments of one state with the keywords of another.  Readers of
``repr()``, ``partial()``, ``__reduce__()`` and calls now take all four
mutable fields as one snapshot under the object's critical section.
