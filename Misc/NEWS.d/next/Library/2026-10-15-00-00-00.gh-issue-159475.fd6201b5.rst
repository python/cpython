:func:`email.utils.parseaddr` and :func:`email.utils.getaddresses` now
reject, in the default ``strict=True`` mode, addresses in which a comment
appears immediately before an atom (i.e. a comment embedded inside a
dot-atom).  Such comments were previously silently dropped and the
surrounding atoms spliced together: ``parseaddr('u(comment)ser@d.com')``
returned ``('comment comment', 'user@d.com')`` even though the strict parser
rejects the address.  The historical lenient behavior remains available via
``strict=False``.
