Fix the ``hz`` codec sometimes encoding ``~`` as a single ``~`` in text that
mixes ASCII and Chinese characters, and the incremental ``iso2022_*`` codecs
mishandling an escape sequence split across calls or keeping the previous
character set after :meth:`!reset`.  Also fix possible crashes in the CJK
incremental and stream codecs when their ``errors`` attribute or their class's
``codec`` attribute is replaced while they are in use.
