Fix a crash in :mod:`xml.parsers.expat` when the
:meth:`~xml.parsers.expat.xmlparser.DefaultHandler` or
:meth:`~xml.parsers.expat.xmlparser.DefaultHandlerExpand` callback raises an
exception or unsets the handler while a token longer than 1024 bytes, such as
a comment, is being reported from a document that is not encoded in UTF-8.
