On platforms where libc provides the ``timezone``, ``daylight`` and
``tzname`` variables, :data:`time.timezone`, :data:`time.daylight` and
:data:`time.tzname` are now taken from libc instead of being guessed from
samples of the local time in January and July.  This fixes stale values for
zones whose rules change during the year, such as ``America/Vancouver`` in
2026.  Standard and DST are now classified by libc rather than by comparing
offsets, so for zones with negative DST such as ``Europe/Dublin``,
:data:`time.tzname` is now ``('IST', 'GMT')`` and :data:`time.timezone` is
``-3600``, matching :attr:`~time.struct_time.tm_isdst`.  On some platforms
:data:`time.daylight` may now be nonzero for zones that observed DST only in
the past.  When libc does not provide ``altzone`` (including glibc and
Windows), :data:`time.altzone` is now found by sampling the current year for
a DST offset instead of assuming a one-hour shift, and equals
:data:`time.timezone` for zones without DST.
