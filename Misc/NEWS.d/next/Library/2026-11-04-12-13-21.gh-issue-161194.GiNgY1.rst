On platforms where libc provides the ``timezone``, ``daylight`` and
``tzname`` variables, :data:`time.timezone`, :data:`time.daylight` and
:data:`time.tzname` are now taken from libc instead of being guessed from
samples of the local time in January and July.  This fixes stale values for
zones whose rules change during the year, such as ``America/Vancouver`` in
2026.  :data:`time.altzone` now equals :data:`time.timezone` for zones
without DST when libc does not provide ``altzone``.
