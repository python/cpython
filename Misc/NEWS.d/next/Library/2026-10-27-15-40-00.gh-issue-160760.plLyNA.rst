The :mod:`asyncio` event loop now runs callbacks scheduled by other
callbacks eagerly, before polling for I/O again, within limits set by the
new :class:`asyncio.EventLoopSettings` and :meth:`loop.set_settings()
<asyncio.loop.set_settings>`. :func:`asyncio.sleep` with a delay of 0 or less
ends eager processing in the current iteration of the loop.
