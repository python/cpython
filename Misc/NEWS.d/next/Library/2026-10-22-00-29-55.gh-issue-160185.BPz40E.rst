Fix :meth:`codecs.StreamWriter.reset` of the stateful CJK codecs (``hz`` and
the ``iso2022_*`` family) not writing the sequence that returns the output to
ASCII.  ``seek(0)`` of :class:`codecs.StreamWriter`,
:class:`codecs.StreamReaderWriter` and :class:`codecs.StreamRecoder` now
resets the writer before moving the stream position.
