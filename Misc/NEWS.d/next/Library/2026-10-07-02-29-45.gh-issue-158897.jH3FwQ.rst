Optimize :meth:`!io.BufferedReader.readline` when lines are close to the
buffer size (128 kB by default) or longer than the buffer size. Replace the
C loop searching for the newline byte in the buffer with a :c:func:`!memchr`
call which is more efficient. In the good case, it makes ``readline()`` up to
4x faster! Patch by Victor Stinner.
